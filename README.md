## 🌿 Wombs of Wisdom

### Her Voice. Her Wisdom. Her Legacy.

Wombs of Wisdom is an AI-powered digital platform designed to preserve, share and continue African women's traditional knowledge across generations.

The platform connects ancestral wisdom with modern technology by allowing elders and communities to contribute knowledge, stories, traditions and lived experiences, while enabling younger women to access this knowledge through an AI-powered conversational companion known as **Gogo**.

> **Preserving the wisdom of yesterday. Empowering the women of today. Inspiring the generations of tomorrow.**

---

## 🌍 The Problem

Across Africa, generations of traditional knowledge have historically been passed down orally from grandmother to mother to daughter.

This includes:

- 🌿 Traditional healing practices
- 🤱 Maternal and women's wisdom
- 🍲 Nutritional knowledge
- 🌱 Knowledge about natural products and plants
- 🧓 Life lessons and advice
- 🗣️ Oral traditions and stories
- 🎨 Cultural practices and identity
- 🌍 Local language and cultural knowledge

However, this knowledge is increasingly at risk of being lost.

Urbanisation, migration, changing family structures, language loss and the passing of elders can create gaps between generations.

As a result, younger women may no longer have access to the knowledge that was traditionally shared within families and communities.

At the same time, much of the information that has been formally documented has been recorded through external institutions and is often inaccessible, written in non-local languages, or separated from the cultural context in which the knowledge originated.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

Wombs of Wisdom creates a digital bridge between generations.

Elders and communities can contribute their knowledge in their own voices and languages. This information can then be stored within the platform and made accessible to younger generations.

At the centre of the platform is **Gogo**, an AI-powered conversational companion that allows users to interact with preserved knowledge through a natural conversation.

Instead of searching through a traditional database, a user can simply ask:

> "Gogo, what did our elders traditionally use for stomach discomfort?"

Gogo searches the knowledge preserved within Wombs of Wisdom and uses the relevant information to generate a conversational response.

The platform is designed to distinguish between:

- Traditional knowledge
- Community knowledge
- Scientific evidence
- Information where evidence is limited or unavailable

This distinction is important because traditional practices should not automatically be presented as scientifically proven medical treatments.

---

# 👵🏾 Meet Gogo

Gogo is the AI-powered conversational component of Wombs of Wisdom.

She is designed to communicate like a caring African grandmother rather than a generic chatbot.

### Gogo's personality

Gogo is:

- Caring
- Warm
- Protective
- Wise
- Encouraging
- Respectful
- Honest about uncertainty
- Firm when necessary

Her conversational style includes natural phrases such as:

> "My child..."

> "Ah, my child..."

> "Listen to Gogo..."

> "Come, let us think about this together."

Gogo's purpose is not to replace family members, elders or healthcare professionals.

Her purpose is to make preserved knowledge easier to access while keeping the cultural context and distinction between tradition and scientific evidence.

# ✨ Key Features

## 👵🏾 Ask Gogo

Users can have a conversation with Gogo and ask questions about knowledge stored within Wombs of Wisdom.

Gogo uses information from the platform's knowledge database to ground her responses.

---

## 🌿 Traditional Knowledge Preservation

Knowledge contributed by elders and communities can be stored digitally instead of relying solely on oral transmission.

Knowledge can include:

- Traditional practices
- Natural products
- Cultural practices
- Stories
- Life lessons
- Nutritional traditions
- Maternal wisdom
- Community experiences

---

## 🌍 Multilingual Support

Wombs of Wisdom is designed with African languages in mind.

The platform aims to allow users to:

- Contribute knowledge in local languages
- Ask Gogo questions in different languages
- Translate preserved knowledge
- Maintain the cultural context of the original contribution

The long-term goal is to support a growing number of African languages.

---

## 🔊 Gogo Voice

The platform is being developed to allow users to listen to Gogo's responses rather than only reading them.

A voice player can appear above each Gogo response:

**▶ Listen to Gogo**

This allows the experience to reflect the oral nature of the knowledge being preserved.

The long-term vision is to make the interaction feel closer to speaking with an elder rather than interacting with a traditional text-based chatbot.
